The Federalist Papers

(2025 Edition)

Article No. 4

"Countries that are divided often invite attacks from others. A strong united government makes the nation more secure and respected.

By working together under one government, citizens benefit from protection and stability that separate states cannot provide."

Adapted by K. Hamilton
