The Federalist Papers

(2025 Edition)

Article No. 1

"The main purpose of these papers is to explain why a strong central government is necessary.

Right now, the states alone can’t solve all our problems. If we want peace, safety, and a fair system that treats everyone equally, we need a stronger government that can manage big issues for the whole country."

Adapted by K. Hamilton
