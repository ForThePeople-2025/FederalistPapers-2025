The Federalist Papers (2025)

Article No. 1

Adapted by K. Hamilton

The main purpose of these papers is to explain why a strong central government is necessary. Right now, the states alone can’t solve all our problems. If we want peace, safety, and a fair system that treats everyone equally, we need a stronger government that can manage big issues for the whole country.
