The Federalist Papers

(2025 Edition)

Article No. 2

"It’s important for the states to stick together as one nation. If they stay separate, they’ll be weak against enemies, and trading or traveling between them will be messy.

A united government keeps the country safe and helps everyone benefit fairly from laws and protections."

Adapted by P. Madison
