The Federalist Papers

(2025 Edition)

Article No. 3

"A united country makes it easier to handle foreign threats. If the states act alone, they might get into wars or conflicts that hurt everyone.

By having a strong central government, the nation can defend itself and maintain peace better than the states could individually."

Adapted by M. Jay
